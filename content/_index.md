**Hello**! I am a cosmologist, working at [APC (AstroParticule et Cosmologie)](https://apc.u-paris.fr/APC_CS/) with Professor Josquin Errard.
Before that, I was a postdoctoral research associate at Cardiff University, with Professor Erminia Calabrese.
I am observing the Cosmic Microwave Background (CMB), the left-over radiation from the Big-Bang, to better understand the origin and content of our Universe.
I am a member of the [ACT](https://act.princeton.edu/), [Simons Observatory (SO)](https://simonsobservatory.org) and [CCAT-prime](http://www.ccatobservatory.org) collaborations.
